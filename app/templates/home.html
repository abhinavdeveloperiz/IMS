Your Trusted Partner in Global Logistics

Welcome to Infra Modern Solution Company (IMS), a dynamic freight forwarding and logistics provider headquartered in Saudi Arabia. With over 15 years of industry expertise, we deliver seamless, end-to-end transportation and supply chain solutions built on operational excellence and customer-centric approach.

We specialize in connecting Saudi Arabia to the world and the world to Saudi Arabia, offering a comprehensive spectrum of logistics services tailored to meet your unique business needs. At IMS, we provide complete transportation solutions across all modes—air, sea, LCL, charter services, and GCC-wide road transportation.

Our state-of-the-art warehousing and distribution services ensure your cargo is handled with precision and efficiency at every stage. Whether you require global imports, exports, or regional GCC logistics, IMS delivers reliable, cost-effective, and timely solutions.

Our mission is to empower businesses by streamlining logistics operations and fostering growth. With commitment to innovation, reliability, and sustainability, Infra Modern Solution Company is your trusted partner for all logistics needs—from local to global.

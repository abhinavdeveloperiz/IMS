Your Trusted Partner in Global Logistics

Welcome to Infra Modern Solution Company (IMS), a dynamic freight forwarding and logistics provider headquartered in Saudi Arabia, dedicated to delivering seamless, end-to-end transportation and supply chain solutions. With over 15 years of industry expertise, IMS is built on a foundation of deep knowledge, operational excellence, and a customer-centric approach.

We specialize in connecting Saudi Arabia to the world and the world to Saudi Arabia, offering a full spectrum of logistics services tailored to meet the diverse needs of our clients. At IMS, we provide comprehensive transportation solutions across all modes—air, sea, LCL (Less than Container Load), charter services, and GCC-wide road transportation.

Complementing our transport capabilities, we offer state-of-the-art warehousing and distribution services, ensuring your cargo is handled with precision and efficiency at every stage of the supply chain. Whether you require global imports, exports, or regional logistics within the GCC, IMS is equipped to deliver reliable, cost-effective, and timely solutions.

Our mission is to empower businesses by streamlining their logistics operations, fostering growth, and ensuring their goods move effortlessly across borders. With a commitment to innovation, reliability, and sustainability, Infra Modern Solution Company is your trusted partner for all your logistics needs, from local to global. Choose IMS—where modern solutions meet global ambitions.
